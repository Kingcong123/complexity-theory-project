Complexity Classes and the Strong Church–Turing Thesis

This page will cover the concept of quantum circuit complexity, which is used to measure the complexity of a quantum circuit. Using this, we will introduce some basic complexity classes related to quantum circuits. Finally, we will cover the concept of the Strong Church–Turing Thesis, and how it relates to quantum computing. This should provide some motivation for learning about Grover's Algorithm.
