Bits and Qubits

This page will cover the basic concepts of bits and qubits, and how they are used in quantum computing. We will introduce a rigorous mathematical mode for bits and qubits, using randomized states as motivation/as a basis. We will also cover the concept of superposition, entanglement, and Dirac notation.
